About Our Studio

Our story

We tell stories with elegance, feeling, and authenticity.

At Aster & Ember Studio, we believe every Pakistani wedding is a layered story of rituals, affection, and celebration. Our work blends documentary honesty with elevated portraiture, resulting in images that feel both intimate and timeless.

From Mehndi evenings lit by warm neon colors to the solemnity of Nikkah and the glamour of Barat and Walima, we are present for every emotional beat and detail that turns a family event into a legacy story.

  • Thoughtful, experience-led wedding coverage.
  • Cinematic production with a personal editorial eye.
  • Respect for cultural detail and family traditions.
